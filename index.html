Why Methane Matters

Methane (CH₄) is a potent greenhouse gas responsible for around 30% of the current rise in global temperature.

Primary Sources:
• Agriculture: Livestock (enteric fermentation) and rice cultivation.
• Energy: Oil, natural gas, and coal mining operations.
• Waste: Landfills and wastewater treatment.

GWP Limits: Global Warming Potential (GWP) measures how much energy the emissions of 1 ton of a gas will absorb over a given period, relative to the emissions of 1 ton of CO₂. Over 20 years, methane is 84 times more potent than CO₂.
